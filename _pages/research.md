## Undergraduate honors thesis

**Capturing Democratic Institutions in Peru: When Parties and Businesses Merge into a Perverse Emporium**
Davidson College, 2025 · Supervised by Dr. Katherine Bersch · *High Honors in Political Science (awarded May 2026)*

I examine how political parties and private interests captured regulatory institutions in Peru, with Congress and higher-education reform as the central case. That capture weakened oversight and reshaped policy outcomes. The fieldwork in Lima included **13 semi-structured interviews** with members of Congress, congressional staff, scholars and journalists. It was funded by a **$4,000 Davidson Research Initiative grant**.

## Applied research

- **School debate in Peru (2025).** Research team, Liga Peruana de Debate Escolar (LPDE). We analyzed 1,447 participations in 16 school tournaments. Women participate at high rates and perform on par with men, and private schools are heavily concentrated. Public-school students compete at the same level once they get access. Co-authored with Luis Valverde and Felipe Zenteno.
- **Chinese diasporas and firms in Africa (2025).** Research Assistant to Dr. Keren Zhu, Davidson College. Systematic literature reviews on how infrastructure projects import institutional capacity.
- **Access to career services (2022–2023).** Data & DEI Research Assistant, Davidson Career Development Center. Reports on how different student groups use the center's services.
- **Oral histories of U.S. diplomacy (2023).** Intern, Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training, Washington DC.
